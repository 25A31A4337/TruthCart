How the shopping checks work

A practical guide to the main features.

①Ask a clear questionInclude the exact product model and the claim you want to check, such as battery life, water resistance, charging speed or warranty.
②Read the evidence labelVerified means the demo evidence supports the claim; Partly means context or limitations matter; Unverified means reliable evidence is missing.
③Compare trade-offsUse price, features, trust labels and unknowns together. A lower price alone does not make a product the best choice.
④Keep a recordSave products, use favorites and alerts, revisit history, and export your shopping list for later review.

About the data in this prototype

This is an interactive frontend demo. Catalog prices, price history, review-risk signals, trust scores and source examples are illustrative unless a real source is explicitly connected. The prototype does not currently fetch live retailer listings or independently verify real-world product claims.

For a production version, connect a backend and trusted product sources, store source URLs and timestamps, and show which evidence supports each claim. Never treat an illustrative score as a measured result.
